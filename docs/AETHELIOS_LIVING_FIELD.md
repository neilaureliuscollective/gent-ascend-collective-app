## Founder visual correction — 2026-09-27

The earlier ambient field preserved the existing dark sphere and removed its scroll reveal. Founder comparison with the Aethelios private command orb showed that this missed the designed, breathing surface inside the sphere and lost the chapter's cinematic travel. This revision adapts the private `app/personal/living-orb.tsx` shader from the founder's Aethelios `aethelios/command-environment-20260927` branch into a public emerald and gold presentation with flowing contours, internal light, slow rotation and breathing. It renders no personal data and does not change the member renderer. A static patterned orb is always available for Still, reduced motion, WebGL unavailability or context loss.

GSAP ScrollTrigger again directs the scene while scrolling: the visual camera moves and enlarges, the architecture gains depth, and signals align. The shader's scroll uniform adds a controlled change in perspective while its time uniform keeps moving after scroll stops. A chapter-local IntersectionObserver pauses ambient animation offscreen; the renderer also stops its frame loop when hidden or out of view. [GSAP ScrollTrigger](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/), [MDN WebGL best practices](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGL_API/WebGL_best_practices), [MDN reduced motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/%40media/prefers-reduced-motion), and [web.dev compositor animation guidance](https://web.dev/articles/stick-to-compositor-only-properties-and-manage-layer-count) informed the bounded scene.

The narrow layout retains a separate art frame above the copy. The background is a dimensional emerald chamber with faint perspective lines and gold arcs. Acceptance now includes continuous internal animation, scroll-driven camera progression, copy separation at 344/768/1440, Still and reduced-motion fallback, no horizontal overflow, and browser error checks. ## Connected life sequence — 2026-09-27

The six life domains now enter from the edges as the visitor scrolls: You, Health, Rituals, Goals, Work and Family. A measured SVG curve runs from each label toward the orb; its illuminated stroke draws in after the label, then a centered gold and emerald halo resolves the relationships into one visual system. A small current continues along the completed paths while the chapter is visible, complementing the orb's ongoing internal motion. The orb brightens gradually as the network assembles. These are conceptual domains, not live account connections or inferred personal information.

The geometry is measured from the actual labels after layout and recomputed on resize, so the connections follow the separate narrow-screen arrangement. A radial mask keeps strokes off the orb's surface. ScrollTrigger scrubs entry, path draw and convergence; it reverses when scrolling back. Still mode, absent choreography and reduced-motion layouts show the complete network as a static illustration. The decorative current stops when the chapter leaves view or Still mode is selected.
