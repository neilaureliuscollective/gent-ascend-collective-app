# The Ascend Journey — Phase 3

Founder authorized September 27, 2026. This slice makes the existing ritual chapter human before it becomes a product study. It extends Phase 2 and does not activate merchandise checkout, pricing, inventory, new product claims or a private routine.

## Experience

The visitor leaves the spatial LifeOS and enters a believable morning preparation moment at a mirror. That composition gives way to the Vitalis concept object on dark mineral material. The product is introduced as one physical expression of deliberate care, with an immediate path to its existing optional 3D atelier and a direct path to the collection. The existing five-product selector remains an editorial preview beneath the directed scene, now on a quieter material environment instead of the ornate gallery backdrop. Shop remains in the global navigation and at the opening threshold.

Desktop holds the two beats in one scene across 2.7 viewport heights. On phones, open Fold widths, short screens, Still and reduced-motion, both images and their copy are complete panels in normal document flow. Inactive desktop beats are inert and hidden from assistive technology; native scrolling, links and chapter anchors remain functional. The scene owns its own discrete ScrollTrigger and refreshes when the responsive layout changes. There is no additional canvas, 3D engine, video or audio. The existing Vitalis atelier loads its renderer only after explicit interaction on `/shop/vitalis#atelier`.

## Asset and provenance

`public/media/world/ritual-mirror-v1.webp` is a 91 KB concept campaign visualization generated with the built-in image generation tool on September 27, 2026, converted to WebP for delivery. Prompt: a believable adult man with a full head of dark hair and a large carefully groomed beard at a dark stone mirror, one hand adjusting his beard, modern obsidian/emerald architecture, restrained gold light, realistic material and skin, dark negative space left for HTML; no products, logos, text, holograms, Louisiana trees or extra people. It is not a member, customer, venue, clinical depiction or final product photograph. Existing `ritual.webp`, `ritual-mobile.webp` and Vitalis packaging studies remain clearly labeled as concept imagery.

## Research and pressure test

The existing direct Three.js atelier makes homepage duplicate 3D wasteful. CSS image compositions provide the material detail at far lower GPU cost; the opt-in object study remains a destination for interested visitors. [GSAP ScrollTrigger](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/) supports held scroll scenes and refresh; [Next Image](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/components/image) supports responsive sizing and lazy images. [Baymard product-finding research](https://baymard.com/research/homepage-and-category-usability) supports preserving direct catalog navigation alongside authored discovery. [Reduced motion guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/%40media/prefers-reduced-motion) supports the complete nonchoreographed path. No claim of conversion lift is made without real traffic.

## Acceptance and limits

Check forward/reverse scroll, chapter anchors, both destination links, mobile and open Fold flow, Still/reduced motion, keyboard focus, no sideways overflow, and the `#atelier` destination. Physical Fold closed/open, Samsung Internet, iPhone Safari, field LCP/INP/CLS and commercial product approval remain separate release gates. The catalog is still an honest preview with no order action. The Reserve and Collective stories and the continuous public-to-auth transition are future phases.
