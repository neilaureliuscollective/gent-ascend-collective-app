# Product vision

Aurelius Collective is a personal operating environment centered on the individual, inclusive of men and women. Aurelius connects a person's goals, actions, observations and progress over time. Atlas is not part of this product.

The roadmap spans physical health, physique, strength, training, nutrition, sleep, recovery, wearables, labs, hormones, metabolic health, provider coordination, genetics, skin, hair/beard, appearance, mental wellbeing, habits, routines, work, finances, relationships, community, family memories and legacy. These are capabilities of one coherent environment, not 25 unrelated apps.

A future digital twin is a projection of validated body observations, scan artifacts and their uncertainty. Quick estimates and precision measurements remain distinguishable. The renderer must not own the person's data.

Community emphasizes finite, purposeful participation: milestones, encouragement, challenges, curated contributions and shared expertise. No infinite engagement feed. Rewards must not imply that favorable reviews are required.

Commerce is a member experience with offers and benefits. Public product commerce may remain a separate site. Shopify is not a dependency. Stripe subscriptions are expected; approximately $99 and $199–229 are business hypotheses, not code rules. Labs, drugs and physical services may have separate charges.

The first product loop: enter easily → identify a goal → record a useful observation/action → see progress → ask Aurelius to interpret authorized context → choose the next action. Start with founder use, then invite selected testers once this loop works. No public teaser/waitlist launch before a usable V1.

Stage 1 builds the shell, ownership and test harness, not a medical platform or working AI. Future health programs require licensed partners and an explicit regulated-domain readiness decision.
