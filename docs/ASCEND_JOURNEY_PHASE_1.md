# The Ascend Journey — Phase 1

Founder approved September 27, 2026. This branch extends `cinematic-estate` and should be reviewed after that branch's existing work. It changes public presentation only. No product, booking, identity, billing, AI service or private OS capability is activated.

## Finished slice

Threshold → The Man → Aethelios → the Ascend Loop, followed by the existing ritual, collection, Reserve, legacy and invitation. The opening no longer uses the Louisiana pavilion. Louisiana remains in the Reserve and final legacy scene. Direct Shop and member entrance links are available at the threshold and through the existing header; the chapter index links to each scene.

The man is a campaign visualization, not a real member or a product data model. The Aethelios visual reuses `AureliusPresence` and its optional WebGL renderer; it has no private context or simulated conversation state. The six stage loop represents the current direction/action/reflection product and a longer-term adaptation concept. Public copy does not assert that every depicted domain is connected today.

## Architecture

`page.tsx` keeps semantic HTML, native links and crawlable copy. `ascend-journey.css` gives every stage a complete nonanimated presentation. `WorldJourney` uses scoped GSAP/ScrollTrigger timelines only at widths above 900px and heights above 720px with native scroll as the source. Smaller layouts use natural flow and the same material and content. Still/reduced-motion remove the extended scroll stages; the shared orb falls back to its SVG. No global smooth-scroll interception, second renderer, new model or new backend service.

One illustrated man asset is stored in desktop and portrait WebP forms. The source was produced with the built-in image generation tool on September 27, 2026. Prompt: cinematic editorial landscape of a believable tall man with a full head of dark hair and a large well-groomed beard, seen from behind in a dark obsidian architectural void; restrained deep emerald and gold material light, empty space for HTML, no Louisiana tree, holograms, circuits, text or logos. This is conceptual brand artwork. The committed WebP files are 56 KB and 34 KB respectively. The official crest is reused without redrawing.

## Acceptance and limits

Required review: threshold CTA and Shop/member exits; full sequence and reverse scroll at 344, 390, 768, open Fold width and 1440; Still and reduced motion; keyboard focus; no horizontal overflow; graphics fallback/context loss; physical Fold closed/open Chrome and Samsung Internet, plus iPhone Safari. Validate field LCP/INP/CLS and physical scroll pacing before production promotion. The browser suite records these paths but is not a substitute for physical GPU, thermal and battery acceptance.

Phase 2 should deepen the living LifeOS sequence only after Phase 1's visual and device review. Merchandise photography, the Collective's people, physical Reserve imagery and continuous auth transition remain later work.
