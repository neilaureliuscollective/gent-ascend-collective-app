## Budget model (planning assumptions, not public allowances)

An illustrative 20% gross-revenue inference ceiling gives Essential $3.998, Signature $9.998 and Architect $25.80/month before payment fees, hosting, database/storage, preview compute, observability, support and taxes. Adjust based on measured margin and accepted product value. Total COGS = input tokens × input rate + output tokens × output rate + cached-input charges + research/tool fees + images + document processing + sandbox CPU/time + preview builds + storage/egress + payment fees + allocated hosting/support. Record each meter with model/version, owner, feature, reservation, usage and status.

A hypothetical $0.05 conversation and $0.35 image (sensitivity assumptions, not provider prices) yields $4 for 80 calls and $2.80 for eight images. Ten hypothetical coding runs at $2/run consume $20 of the Architect inference ceiling, leaving only $5.80 for other inference. That may be insufficient once sandboxes and support are included. At $5/run those ten jobs alone cost $50; execution must stop before overspending rather than promise unlimited use.

Existing Studio ceiling is 12 rolling-day reservations, not a sustainable new public monthly allowance: 360 images × illustrative $0.35 = $126/month. Preserve established access now, but measure actual model/size costs and obtain approved monthly commercial caps before new launch enrollment. A failed ambiguous image/job may still be billed; do not refund reservations solely from a client timeout. Specialist fan-out and synthesis costs accumulate, even when one visible reply is produced.

Launch ledger requirements: atomic owner/feature/window reservation; concurrent-job cap; maximum per-job spend; measured input/output and provider usage; failure/timeout reconciliation; visible remaining allowance; server-side denial at limit; no client-only counter; no automatic paid overage. Top-ups require explicit user purchase plus signed billing reconciliation. Existing allowances are not silently rewritten here.

## Future product boundaries

CRM: Aethelios-branded business product with contacts/companies/pipelines, approved follow-ups and reporting. Keep organization/customer data separate from personal context; explicit selected-record consent for Intelligence and project-specific inputs for Architect. Evaluate Twenty licensing and HighLevel commercial/API rights, tenancy/export and cost before adoption. No new repository or CRM release.

Alliance: genuine Aethelios-branded distribution, independent canonical accounts, attribution ledger, agreed commission calculation and organization-funded seats. Future auditability/refunds/cancellation/consent required; no platform rebranding, live payouts or healthcare referral compensation.

Ascendance/Enterprise: future release gates and separately priced entitlements; no active purchase option. Enterprise adds real organizations, role/seat policy and isolation before shared context. Managed Architect hosting is an optional separately bounded future service, not unlimited infrastructure included with membership.

## Before a new customer can pay

1. Founder reviews Phase One and Phase Two branches; resolve corporate initial base and GitHub account runner availability.
2. Obtain failed member CI assertions and fix/rerun; real two-person Auth/RLS and billing acceptance in adequate local or dedicated staging infrastructure.
3. Validate actual live-provider capabilities and prices, then approve sustainable allowances and truthful available-feature terms.
4. Extend billing/schema and execution ledger additively for Architect, accept test-mode checkout/webhooks/cancel/upgrade/replay/expired access; preserve historical paid terms.
5. Review ownership, privacy, support/tax and software/physical-commerce separation; approve verified origins and deployment.
6. Founder separately approves new live products and production launch. No code review or preview substitutes for that authorization.
