The public Man chapter now uses three authored concept frames of one consistent man: a wide architectural view, a nearer portrait and a close action at a warm light plane. Native scroll holds each state and crossfades/camera-moves between them in both directions. The six floating topic labels are removed. Static/reduced-motion visitors receive the existing wide keyframe and complete copy.

## Visual acceptance

Inspect at the beginning, middle and end of the Man chapter at closed Fold, unfolded width and desktop. A normal scroll should visibly change the camera from whole man to face to hand/light, not only fade text. Fast and reverse scrolling should show a stable corresponding frame without forcing a duration. The typography remains legible over the dark portion of each frame. This implementation validates direction, not human motion: a genuine living expression and continuous action still require directed footage or a carefully controlled offline render. Founder acceptance on a physical Fold is the next art and performance gate.

## Optional Runway Pro app production guide

The founder can create short clips manually from the existing stills if the concept sequence feels right. Keep a single subject with full dark swept-back hair, a large sharp beard and black tailored clothing. Use the same dark emerald mineral hall and restrained warm gold practical light. Export clean video without titles, logo, music or synthetic interface graphics; preserve a clean first and last frame. Do not combine different faces into one transition.

1. **Wide / arrival:** use `the-man-mobile.webp` as the visual reference. Hold the man almost still for 3–4 seconds; a slow camera push through the hall and natural cloth/breath motion. Portrait framing must preserve full figure and negative space above.
2. **Human / weight:** use `the-man-portrait.webp` as the reference. 3–4 seconds with a subtle change of gaze or breath; keep beard and face identity fixed. The warm architecture moves gently behind him, and he remains grounded rather than distressed.
3. **Decision / light:** use `the-man-decision.webp` as the reference. 3–4 seconds as the hand approaches a physical gold-lit seam; minor finger movement and light change, no magic effect. Keep the hand anatomically believable.

Export portrait and landscape versions or a sufficiently wide protected master that survives both crops. Review for identity drift, melted fingers, jitter and camera warps on the phone. The site should only adopt an approved clip after browser playback, loading and reduced-motion tests; stills remain valid posters and fallback. No API key is needed for this manual workflow.
