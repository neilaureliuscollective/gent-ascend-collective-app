## Motion and rendering budget

No new animation libraries, pointer loops or scene runtimes. Large environmental gradients are static. Gold shimmer runs once on fine-pointer hover; architectural core opacity pulses twice on desktop arrival. Still mode disables animation; reduced motion disables animation/transitions and hover movement. Existing quiet mode pauses the architectural core while writing or in dialogs. Blur is limited to the desktop topbar and requires glass preference and browser support. Narrow/short screens omit decorative horizon geometry; solid/forced-color modes remove atmosphere and restore readable controls.

## Research
