# Aethelios Intelligence Motion — creative build brief

Prepared October 12, 2026. Research and design preparation only. This document proposes the next identity; it does not declare artwork approved, measured performance established, or a new implementation deployed. The founder confirmed that current response visibility works but its abstract ribbon does not feel on brand.

## Decision

Develop the existing internally illuminated Aethelios sphere into a coherent operational presence, framed by precise guiding geometry derived from the official identity. Preserve the seal artwork. Use a recognizable silhouette, shared material treatment and authored physical behavior at every scale. The current ribbon remains the deployed visibility solution until an approved replacement is tested.

The desired character is composed, intelligent, deliberate, sophisticated and responsive. It reflects a digital co-founder who supports human judgment. Motion should communicate attention and work, without implying consciousness, hidden knowledge, autonomous authority or a connection that does not exist.

## Why the current treatment falls short

The ribbon uses the palette but lacks a defensible connection to the architectural A, guiding star, circular seal or existing sphere. Most busy states share similar movement. Functional visibility and state tests establish usability, not a distinctive brand identity. Improving color or adding more particles would not resolve these problems.

## Three authored directions to compare

These are briefs for the first build, not three fabricated finished assets.

1. **Sphere with guiding aperture — recommended.** A deep emerald sphere with visible internal light and a fine surrounding aperture. The stable form expresses continuity; light gathering, direction and aperture tension express the operation. The gold accent stays precise. Best balance of identity, recognizable states and mobile scalability.
2. **Sphere with aligned internal contours.** Keep the sphere as the complete silhouette. Form two or three controlled contours inside it using the A's directional structure and central light. Quieter and closer to current artwork; test whether internal detail remains legible at response scale.
3. **Guiding aperture with a concentrated core.** A more graphic interpretation for compact tasks: a circular boundary, central focus and deliberate open/closed tension. Potentially excellent at small sizes, but test carefully against a generic spinner. It must share the large sphere's geometry and materials.

Select one primary direction and derive its compact version. Do not deploy different unrelated identities in different rooms. Recommendation: direction 1, with direction 3 used only as a scale reduction if the silhouette and behavior remain recognizably related.

## Material and lighting specification

- Environment: obsidian `#080D0D`, graphite reading planes and emerald shadow `#102B26`.
- Body: imperial emerald `#174438` and active emerald `#236A52`, with limited luminous detail from `#36896C` / `#7BE7B1`.
- Accent: Reserve Gold `#C4912F`, warm highlight `#E0BB6A`; never a dominant gold fill.
- Text: existing semantic text tokens; motion cannot become the background of reading text.
- One consistent upper-left material light and a controlled internal focal light. Surface shading should show depth, not resemble glossy plastic or chrome.
- Dark areas remain meaningful. Bloom stays local to the silhouette; no full-screen flashing or excessive blur. Compact versions simplify detail rather than shrinking a complex scene until it is illegible.
- These are art-direction proposals. Shader values, contour control points and exact construction ratios must be authored and recorded after visual comparison; they are not claimed exact measurements of the crest.

## Motion physics

Use three physical verbs: **gather**, **direct**, **resolve**. Incoming attention gathers inward; producing an answer directs energy outward; acknowledged completion resolves into a quiet stable form. Searching uses controlled directional inspection. Saving uses a precise material accent, not a circular percentage fill.

Starting timing proposals for the study: 160–240ms state interpolation; 320–500ms one-shot completion; 3–6s indeterminate internal circulation. These are creative starting points to validate, not measured perception standards. Request feedback begins immediately. Do not hold back text, extend a request or invent intermediate phases to showcase animation. Transitions retarget from their current pose when an operation interrupts them.

Idle and terminal states do not run continuous activity loops. Keep the spherical silhouette stable; movement is primarily internal. Audio deformation must be restrained enough that the object stays identifiable. Scale and deformation cannot cause layout shifts or change touch targets.

## Complete operation vocabulary

Appearance is a proposal; event authority is a requirement. See the engineering brief for actual source availability.

| Phase       | Intended behavior                                      | Truth boundary                                                                         |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ready       | Quiet, recognizable core                               | No implied ongoing intelligence work                                                   |
| loading     | Low-energy circulation within unresolved aperture      | Actual read/load request only                                                          |
| connecting  | A bounded approach toward alignment                    | Actual connection attempt; not a claim of success                                      |
| waiting     | Restrained internal illumination without convergence   | Awaiting response or approved execution; no estimated completion                       |
| thinking    | Internal contours gather and organize                  | Observed reasoning event only, never prompt/time inference                             |
| searching   | Directional sweep through a stable core                | Observed provider search; no imaginary search nodes                                    |
| researching | Broader deliberate inspection pattern                  | Only when an operation owner exposes an actual research state                          |
| generating  | Focused outward light while content emerges            | Observed output/stream; no token-by-token flashing                                     |
| processing  | Ordered internal circulation                           | An actual local or server operation with this observation                              |
| listening   | Inward contour response to measured input envelope     | Granted, active microphone input; zero fake syllables                                  |
| speaking    | Outward contour response to measured playback envelope | Actual playback phase; blocked playback cannot animate as audible speech               |
| saving      | Narrow gold accent with unresolved core                | Persistence in flight; no completion flourish                                          |
| complete    | One short alignment/highlight, then settle             | Saved only when persistence is confirmed; generic Complete for operations with no save |
| failed      | Activity stops, focus relaxes; readable explanation    | Distinguish failed operation from uncertain persistence                                |
| cancelled   | Controlled withdrawal, then stillness                  | Do not imply that partial work was successfully saved                                  |

Muted microphone, blocked playback, session ended, task awaiting approval, declined task and uncertain save are explicit modifiers/labels. They must not be visually overwritten by a prettier animation. Missing audio measurements mean no envelope movement, not synthetic sound.

## Scale and placement

Starting size bands: compact 32–48px; primary response 96–144px; immersive Live 240–320px. These are layout proposals, not mandatory physical-device dimensions. Each form must be recognizable in grayscale and Still mode. Do not rely on an icon in the toolbar as the primary feedback.

- Talk: at the forming answer before headers; retain the current long-reply visibility behavior. Reading and composer remain usable.
- Work/company conversations: with the active response. Deliverable operations: beside the active task or review controls, visible if the editor is scrolled.
- Studio images: beside the actual creation action/result area. A shared page-top indicator alone is insufficient when the brief is long.
- Narration, footage, storyboard, campaign, finish and delivery: local to the initiating controls or request record. Test scrolled layouts. Distinguish download, local packaging, provider request, queued record and confirmed save.
- Live: one focal immersive presence and a related compact presence during navigation. Background tasks have their own bounded status marks and never masquerade as the voice phase.
- Entities: one primary Aethelios presence plus compact contribution marks. Animate per-Entity execution only if real contribution events exist; selected names or approved cast alone are not proof of current work.
- Queued and approval records: static, clearly labeled. Decorative links are not evidence of running agents or connected services.
- Homepage and public cinematic artwork: outside this build. Preserve current brand assets and scene behavior.

## First build deliverables

Produce three isolated, clearly labeled motion studies in actual Talk and Live layouts, plus one compact Work/Studio comparison. No account, model call, microphone or production mutation is required for this study.

For each direction deliver: construction board; static/monochrome/forced-colors variants; all 15 phase frames; authored keyframes or procedural parameters; full-motion and Still playback; transition examples; source geometry and material tokens; mobile/desktop screenshots; and a short explanation of its connection to Aethelios.

A scripted visual study may have a timed sequence only when prominently labeled as a synthetic preview. Include waiting → observed-thinking fixture → generation → saving → confirmed completion; listening/speaking using an explicitly illustrative level slider; interrupted transitions; failure/cancellation/uncertainty; and a long scrolled answer. Render at 344, 390, 768 and 1440px, with larger text and keyboard-constrained height.

## Creative review and release criteria

The founder must see the moving concepts, not approve an adjective-filled description. Ask whether the silhouette belongs beside the official seal, whether the material feels appropriate, whether the states differ coherently, and whether it remains composed through a 30-second wait and a long answer. A small formative recognition exercise is useful, but must not be called statistically validated research.

Select and approve the actual moving identity before connecting the replacement to production. Current visibility, real state plumbing, Talk/voice behavior and recovery remain intact during preparation. Technical checks cannot certify premium aesthetic quality; both creative approval and engineering acceptance are required.
