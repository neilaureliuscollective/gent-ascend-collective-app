## Revenue architecture and next phases

Shopify remains catalog, price, stock, cart, discount, checkout, order and fulfillment authority. Supabase remains Aethelios identity, preferences, owned saved choices and product intelligence state. Stripe software subscriptions and existing entitlement policy are independent of Shopify commerce. No new shopping backend or migration.

First revenue gate is approved assortment and tested guest checkout, followed by founder review/release and an instrumented discovery-to-checkout funnel. For actual revenue attribution, choose the authorized analytics sink and consent contract; ingest verified Shopify order/paid/refund events only after merchant app/webhook setup. Verify raw-body HMAC, deduplicate provider IDs, bind shop and trusted checkout attribution, reconcile cancellations/refunds/currencies, and avoid treating a return URL/browser event as proof of payment. Never send private order contents to general analytics or AI by default.

Read-only Talk product discovery should then use bounded approved catalog tools returning stable Shopify IDs, source/fetchedAt, current price/currency/availability and approved formulation facts. Compare only real records; separate missing information. Routine suggestions are optional, nonclinical and avoid unsupported medical benefits. Prepare carts only on explicit requests and review exact variants/quantities/current Shopify quote before mutation. Reorder requires verified customer/order provenance and current catalog/price/availability review. Replenishment requires explicit opt-in and verified purchase/usage evidence, never wishlist inference. Specialized Entities receive only the minimum authorized catalog or owner-bound commerce data; no financial/clinical/founder context expansion.

Member economics remains a proposal. Resolve real active entitlement server-side, link a verified Shopify buyer with consent, select an eligible merchant-approved discount mechanism, and obtain Shopify-confirmed allocations. Quote receipt should record shop, owner/buyer binding, real variants/quantity/currency, fetchedAt/expiry, public price, actual discount allocation, policy version and provider cart reference server-side. Revalidate identity/entitlement/price/availability at checkout; explain changed offers. Test revocation, expiry, stacking/exclusions, markets and exact checkout totals before advertising benefits. Existing Stripe tiers do not authorize a displayed member price, loyalty credit, bundle or early-access purchase.

Future merchant portal: first-party Headless credentials are a single-store operational model. Independently owned stores need an appropriately distributed Shopify app, merchant installation/authorization and minimal scoped Admin access, protected data approval where needed, encrypted per-shop token lifecycle, uninstall/compliance webhooks, tenant isolation and merchant-controlled publication. Custom distribution serves a specific eligible merchant; broad independent merchant onboarding requires public distribution/review. Verify these constraints before selecting implementation. Merchant stories/content-quality reports may stage drafts but never publish silently. Analytics and specialized Entities are shop-scoped. No multi-store cart, seller payouts or speculative marketplace ledger.
